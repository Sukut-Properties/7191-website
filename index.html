Before You Arrive

Help us serve you faster by being prepared.

1

Cover Your Load

Secure tarps or covers to prevent debris on the road.

2

Sort Materials

Separate clean dirt from mixed materials when possible for best pricing.

3

Cut Rebar Tight

Rebar must be cut close to concrete. Excess rebar increases price.

4

Have Payment Ready

Cash (exact change), check, or credit card. Checks payable to "71-91, LLC".

5

No Trash or Plastics

Loads with prohibited items will be refused or charged extra.

6

Know Your Load

Be ready to describe your material type and truck size for quick inspection.

Clean dirt is natural soil without any debris, rocks, roots, concrete, asphalt, or other materials mixed in. It should be free of contaminants and ready for reuse or fill.
